03 / Our purpose

A network built
on connection.

Greeks in Robotics is a community initiative to connect people with Greek roots working across robotics. We begin in the United Kingdom, with space to grow into a wider international network.

From physical AI and autonomous systems to medical robots and human–robot interaction, the goal is simple: make it easier to find one another, share expertise, and work together.
